## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an academic, institutional, and research-grade visual identity for cybersecurity awareness and risk-evaluation. Instead of indulging in sensationalist tropes—such as neon greens, terminal monospaces, or shadowy matrix aesthetics—the identity is founded on clarity, dignity, institutional trust, and pedagogical authority.

The visual style blends **Corporate / Modern** precision with warm editorial readability. Designed specifically for students, researchers, institutional workers, and civic organizations operating under constrained infrastructure, the platform balances crisp data hierarchy with comforting, warm surfaces. The aesthetic evokes the authority of a peer-reviewed research institute combined with the seamless usability of modern risk assessment software.

The official brandmark integrates a vigilant analytical eye within a network topology rendered across the geographic silhouette, transitioning smoothly between deep oceanic navy and deep teal. This symbolism guides the platform's overarching design decisions: structural reliability anchored by deep navy, paired with analytical clarity brought forward by balanced teal accents.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around an editorial light mode, avoiding visual fatigue during extensive diagnostic assessments and academic modules.

### Core Canvas & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base (`#F7F6F2`)**: A tactile, warm off-white canvas preventing harsh optical glare and offering paper-like grounding.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure white elevated cards, providing a distinct, pristine layer above the warm background.
- **Surface Border (`#E7E7E2`)**: Subtle, warm hairline boundary defining cards and structured modules without visual clutter.

### Primary Typography & Accents
- **Primary Navy (`#132A3A`)**: The platform's anchor color. Used for structural navigation bars, major headings, key metric indicators, and primary action buttons.
- **Main Body Text (`#1D2935`)**: Deep charcoal-slate ensuring WCAG AAA accessibility across light surfaces without the stark harshness of pure black.
- **Secondary / Metadata Text (`#66727D`)**: Neutral slate for captions, helper text, and secondary academic metadata.
- **Teal Accent (`#159A9C`)**: The core analytical accent. Applied surgically to active tab borders, survey progress meters, primary interactive icons, and chart poly-lines.
- **Interactive Tint (`#F0F9F9`)**: A delicate 8% teal wash used exclusively for selected options, active radio containers, and callout backgrounds.

### Semantic Risk Stratification
- **Low Risk / Safe (`#159A9C` / `#10B981`)**: Deep analytical teal transitioning to crisp emerald. Used for compliant risk outcomes, verified status badges, and passing scores.
- **Medium Risk / Caution (`#F59E0B`)**: Warm amber for advisory alerts, non-critical vulnerabilities, and pending assessments.
- **High Risk / Critical (`#E0564C`)**: A balanced, muted coral red communicating critical exposure without inciting panic.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes clarity, cross-platform stability, and bi-directional bilingual support.

### Font Pairings & Multi-Language Rules
- **Latin Script**: **Inter** handles all Latin headline, body, and micro-label hierarchies. Its geometric construction, tall x-height, and neutral optical rhythm ensure effortless reading across dense evaluation frameworks.
- **Arabic Script**: Paired with **Noto Sans Arabic** (primary) and **IBM Plex Sans Arabic** (alternative institutional display). These typefaces offer proportional alignment with Inter's vertical metric heights, preserving visual harmony in bi-directional layouts without horizontal clipping or broken line-height rhythms.

### Hierarchy & Style Execution
- **Headings**: Styled in tight, authoritative weights (`600` and `700`) with negative tracking to convey structural presence.
- **Body & Questions**: Rendered in `#1D2935` at comfortable reading line heights (`1.5` to `1.55`) to facilitate continuous assessment flows without visual fatigue.
- **System Labels & Overlines**: Uppercase metadata and evaluation tags use `label-sm` with subtle positive letter spacing (`0.04em`) to establish crisp analytical categorization.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an intentional, rhythmic **12-column grid** on desktop (max content width: 1200px) centered within the warm `#F7F6F2` canvas. 

### Breakpoints & Fluid Responsiveness
- **Desktop (1024px and above)**: 12-column grid with `1.5rem` gutters and generous `3rem` section margins. Question modules and risk matrices span 8 to 10 columns for focused visual scanning.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8-column grid with `1.25rem` gutters and `2rem` screen margins. Form controls and question lists collapse to 8 full-width columns.
- **Mobile (below 768px)**: 4-column layout with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` screen margins. Card elevations shift to compact padding (`space-md`) to maximize vertical readability.

### Structural Rhythm
Section padding maintains a rhythmic vertical cadence utilizing `space-xl` (48px) between major thematic cards and `space-md` (20px) between stacked question items or interactive inputs.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects exaggerated, harsh dropshadows in favor of an ambient, layered paper aesthetic that complements academic rigor.

### Surface Hierarchy
1. **Canvas Level (0dp)**: The ambient `#F7F6F2` baseline background.
2. **Elevated Assessment Cards (1dp)**: Pure `#FFFFFF` surfaces defined by a hairline structural border (`1px solid #E7E7E2`) and an ambient, low-density shadow:
   `box-shadow: 0 8px 30px rgba(19, 42, 58, 0.08);`
   The shadow is tinted with the platform's Primary Navy (`#132A3A`) rather than generic black, producing a natural, cohesive ambient atmospheric depth.
3. **Interactive & Hover States (2dp)**: Upon hovering over diagnostic option cards or actionable elements, the card transitions smoothly (`200ms ease-out`):
   `box-shadow: 0 12px 36px rgba(19, 42, 58, 0.11);` with a slight `-1px` transform.
4. **Modals & Flyouts (3dp)**: Critical risk advisories and dialogue overlays use an authoritative, soft focus layer:
   `box-shadow: 0 20px 48px rgba(19, 42, 58, 0.16);` paired with a translucent navy backdrop blur (`rgba(19, 42, 58, 0.4)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`).

## Shapes

The platform employs a contemporary, tailored curvature language that balances approachable interaction with precise institutional dignity.

- **Main Assessment & Diagnostic Cards**: Fixed between `18px` and `22px` border-radius (`1.25rem` or `rounded-xl`). This soft rectangular geometry gives substantive weight and containment to multi-part questionnaire modules.
- **Action Buttons & Inputs**: Standardized at `10px` to `12px` border-radius (`rounded-md` / `rounded-lg`), producing ergonomic, dependable click targets.
- **Risk Indicator Badges & Metadata Chips**: Finished with full-pill radii (`9999px`) to immediately set them apart from structural card content.
- **Selection Radio Checkpoints**: Circular with an inner concentric dot upon activation, reinforcing intuitive touchpoint feedback.

## Components

### 1. Assessment Question Cards & Option Selectors
- **Unselected State**: `#FFFFFF` background, `1.5px solid #E7E7E2` border, `18px` to `20px` border-radius, `space-md` inner padding. Text in `#1D2935`.
- **Selected State**: Light teal wash background (`#F0F9F9` / `rgba(21, 154, 156, 0.08)`), structural `2px solid #159A9C` border, text in `#132A3A`. Accompanied by a solid `#159A9C` check indicator.
- **Hover State**: Subtle border darkening to `#159A9C` (50% opacity) and smooth background shift to `#FAFDFD`.

### 2. Buttons
- **Primary Button**: Filled `#132A3A` Navy background, pure white text, `10px` radius, medium font weight (`600`), horizontal padding `24px`, vertical padding `14px`. Hover state applies `#1B3B52` with zero scale distortion.
- **Secondary / Ghost Button**: Outlined with `1.5px solid #132A3A`, transparent background, `#132A3A` typography. Hover brings an ultra-subtle tint: `rgba(19, 42, 58, 0.05)`.
- **Destructive / High-Risk Button**: Filled `#E0564C` Coral, white text, reserved for clearing diagnostic state or submitting breach reports.

### 3. Progress Meters & Steppers
- **Linear Track**: 6px height, rounded ends, `#E7E7E2` neutral background.
- **Active Fill**: Solid `#159A9C` Teal transition bar with smooth CSS width interpolation.
- **Step Counters**: Numerals formatted in `label-md` with completed steps circled in filled teal `#159A9C`, active step outlined in Navy `#132A3A`, and upcoming steps muted in `#66727D`.

### 4. Risk Evaluation Badges & Status Chips
- **Low Risk**: `#E8F6F6` background with `#159A9C` typography and status dot.
- **Medium Risk**: `#FEF3C7` background with `#B45309` amber typography.
- **High Risk**: `#FDE8E8` background with `#E0564C` muted coral typography.
- **Format**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), `space-xs` vertical and `space-sm` horizontal padding, `12px` font weight `600`.

### 5. Input Fields & Form Controls
- **Text & Numerical Inputs**: `#FFFFFF` surface, `1.5px solid #E7E7E2` default border, `10px` border-radius, `14px` padding. Focus state: `2px solid #159A9C` with subtle `0 0 0 3px rgba(21, 154, 156, 0.15)` focus ring. Placeholder text styled in `#66727D`.
- **Validation Messages**: Positioned 4px below the input; red warnings set in `#E0564C`, informational academic guidance set in `#159A9C`.
